# Terms and Definitions

I have read a lot of Mexican Train Rules.
While the rules are all very similar they use different terms that all ultimately mean the same thing.
For this repository I note the various names for a thing here.
If you see a term that just says **See something else** that is because I tried to use **Something else** throughout these pages.
I am human.
I may have erred in some cases.
If you need to, you may cancel me for the mistake.
(I don't care about cancel culture.  I took this time to do this because I love the game.  Cancel away.  I'll still love the game.)

|Term|Definition|
|-|-|
|Blank|A Face with no Pips (or the number 0).|
|Block|See Derail.|
|Boneyard|Where all Tiles start, are shuffled, and drawn from during play.|
|Boxcar|See Tile.  See also Wagon.|
|Close&nbsp;a&nbsp;Double|See Satisfy a Double.|
|Depot|Central point for the Engine and all player Lines.  Mexican Trains may also start from the Depot.|
|Derail|Laying a Tile that ends in a number with no remaining unplayed Tiles.|
|Domino|See Tile.  Also, the general term in many domino games when one plays the last Tile in a Hand.|
|Double|A Tile where both Faces have the same number of Pips.|
|Doubles Rules|A separate set of rules that supercede regular game play rules when there is an Unsatisfied Double on any Public or Private Train.|
|Draw|Take a Tile from the Boneyard either to form a new Hand or because no Tiles in the Hand can be played.|
|Empties|Tiles remaining in the Hand after player has started his Private Train. Also, after another player goes out, the Tiles remaining in a Hand and whose total is added to the player's score for this Round.|
|End|See Face.|
|Engine|The initial Tile for the current Round.  All Lines start with a Face matching the Engine.|
|Face|Half of a Tile with a number of Pips (or an actual number.)|
|Game|All the Rounds of a particular playing session.|
|Go&nbsp;Out|See Domino general term definition.|
|Hand|The Tiles held by a player in the current Round.|
|Hub|See Depot.|
|Locomotive|See Engine.|
|Line|Tiles laid Face to Face where the Faces of adjacent Tiles match.  All Lines start by matching the Engine Face number forming a Private Train for each Player.  A train is started when a player has a Tile with the left Face matching the number of the Engine. The next Tile must match the right Face of the first Tile in your train.|
|Marked|A private Train that is temporarily Public allowing any player to lay Tiles on it.|
|Marker|A coin or token used to temporarily change a Private Train to a Public Train.|
|Mexican&nbsp;Train|A Public Train that starts with a Tile matching the Engine for the current Round and is always open for any player to lay Tiles on it.  The Mexican Train usually starts near an edge of the Game surface.  Alternately the Mexican Train starts at the Depot and has a permanent special Marker identifying it as the Mexican Train.|
|Open Double|See Unsatisfied Double.|
|Personal&nbsp;Reserve|See Empties.|
|Pip|A dot on a Tile Face that counts as one point in scoring.  Every Tile in the domino set has a unique arrangement of Pips varying from 0:0 to 12:12 (or the highest number in the set used).  A valid set has no duplicate Tiles.  Every combination of Pips and Faces is different.|
|Private&nbsp;Train|A Line owned by a particular player who has exclusive access to lay Tiles against the Line.  Private Trains may temporarily become Public Trains during the Round.|
|Public&nbsp;Train|All Mexican Trains and all Marked Private trains, if any, that are open to all players to lay Tiles on.|
|Round|The play from shuffling the Boneyard, drawing Hands, docking the Engine to the Depot, and laying Tiles until one player empties his Hand.|
|Satisfy&nbsp;a&nbsp;Double|Lay another Tile with a Face matching the Double.|
|Sequence|A set of Tiles that match Face to Face and are played on a Line in a single Turn.|
|Station|See Depot.|
|Tap|An actual tap of one's final Tile that alerts other players one may be able to go out on his next Turn.|
|Tile|A Domino with two Faces.|
|Track|See Line.|
|Train|See Line.|
|Train Yard|See Boneyard.|
|Turn|The act of playing that starts when Laying Tile(s) on a Line(s) and lasts until the last Tile laid is released.|
|Unclosed&nbsp;Double|See Unsatisfied Double.|
|Unsatisfied&nbsp;Double|A Double that has not yet had another Tile laid next to it.|
|Wagon|See Tile (from French Translation of boxcar.)|
|Wild|Treat blank Faces as matching any other Face, including other Blanks.|
|Uno|A Card game unrelated to Dominos, despite this word also being the reason this FAQ exists and needing to define Turn.  See Tap for more information.  (Yes this term is out of alphabetic sequence because it is anathema to Mexican Train.)|
